## ✅ Before you click buy — 7 questions for the seller
1. Which FC (make/model)? → check it's on the ArduPilot supported list
2. Ever crashed? → "flown twice, crashed once" = fine; "rebuilt after a bad crash" = inspect in person
3. Motors spin freely? (ask for a bench-test video)
4. Frame arms crack-free? (carbon hides cracks under paint)
5. Power module included?
6. Prop condition? (buy spares regardless)
7. Total flight hours?
